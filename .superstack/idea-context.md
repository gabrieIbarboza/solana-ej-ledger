### Next Steps

- Build the hackathon MVP exactly around the PRD's narrow path: Core -> API -> SDK -> Solana proof -> simple consumer UI.
- Position the product as "RID executavel e auditavel para Empresas Juniores"; do not lead with blockchain in user interviews.
- Recruit 20 EJ leads and complete 10 conversations with finance/admin, presidents/directors, and members who request reimbursements.
- Ask each EJ for one anonymized RID or policy snippet, one current request/reimbursement artifact, and the last three expense decisions without personal data.
- Convert 5 to 10 real rules from 3 EJs into structured policy JSON and test whether the engine's decisions match the finance/admin decision.
- Seek a concrete commitment before expanding scope: 3 design partners plus at least 1 LOI, paid pilot, or written agreement to run the next reimbursement cycle through the tool.
- Integration-first Solana path: use deterministic hashes plus Solana Memo Program/devnet for proof; use Helius for RPC/indexing; defer Squads until a partner actually wants on-chain treasury.
- Avoid for now: Pix, bank integration, crypto custody, on/off-ramp, OCR, AI parsing of arbitrary RIDs, custom Solana program, swaps, yield, and complete treasury management.
